Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience in real estate?

No. We care more about problem-solving ability, curiosity, collaboration, and execution.

Depending on the role, we support remote, hybrid, and on-site opportunities.

What technologies do you use?

We leverage modern tech (AI, Automation, CRM systems) to optimize property acquisition, sales workflows, and smart home infrastructure. Our stack is chosen to deliver speed and transparency to the real estate market.

How long does the hiring process take?

We aim to keep the process efficient and transparent, typically within a few weeks depending on the role.
